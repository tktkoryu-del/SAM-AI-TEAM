## Editorial safeguards
- Label reenactments and illustrative stock footage so they cannot be mistaken for archival evidence.
- Do not show graphic victim photographs for shock value.
- Do not use original victim photographs unless source, context, dignity, privacy, and reuse rights have been reviewed.
- Do not treat stock footage as evidence of events or places not actually depicted.
- Verify every additional image, sound effect, and music track before publication.
